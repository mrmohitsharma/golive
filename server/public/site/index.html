How it works

Live in 4 steps.

No crew, no van, no cables. Most organisers run their first test show in under half an hour, and the app walks you through every step.

  1. 1

    Create your event

    Sign up and name your event. Choose Cloud OBS (we mix the show on our servers, nothing to install) or Laptop OBS on your own computer.

  2. 2

    Add phone cameras

    Share the camera QR code or link on WhatsApp. Volunteers scan it, name their camera and tap Go live. No accounts, any Android phone or iPhone.

  3. 3

    Choose where viewers watch

    Your own ad-free watch page, YouTube, your Facebook Page, Instagram or any service with a stream key. Pick a main stream and switch on as many extra places as you like.

  4. 4

    Go live

    In the control room: pick a camera, press Take, press Go live. Share the watch link and you're on air everywhere at once.

Questions

What organisers ask us.

What kinds of events can I stream?

Anything with people and phones: tournaments and league matches, weddings and family functions, concerts and fests, conferences and launches, religious events, school and college events. If you can film it on a phone, you can broadcast it.

Do I need special cameras or equipment?

No. Any recent Android phone or iPhone works as a camera. A tripod or clamp helps keep the shot steady. The director needs one phone or tablet.

What internet do the cameras need?

About 2.5 Mbps upload per camera at 720p on 4G, or 1.5 Mbps on a weak signal. With cloud mixing nothing else at the venue needs a connection.

Do viewers need to install anything?

No. They watch on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or your watch page in any browser.

Do I need a YouTube channel?

No. Every event can stream to its own watch page with our ad-free player, which works on any phone or computer and can be embedded on your website. Add YouTube, Facebook or Instagram as well if you like.

What if a camera operator gets a phone call?

On Android the video keeps going and the phone pauses only the sound during the call. If that camera is on air, the show switches to another camera automatically and the director sees ON A CALL. When the operator returns to the app the camera picks up again.

Can viewers play along?

Yes. Quizzes, polls, predictions, spin the wheel and lucky draws pop up on the watch page while the video keeps playing. Viewers earn points, see the Top players and win prizes you set up.

Is it really live?

The director sees the cameras with under a second of delay. Viewers on YouTube and Facebook are usually 10 to 30 seconds behind, which is normal for those platforms.

Does the video go on my own YouTube channel?

Yes. You connect your channel once and every broadcast appears there, so the views, subscribers and recording are yours.

What happens if my credits run out mid-show?

The director is warned at 10 minutes and again at 2 minutes left. If credits reach zero the show keeps going for a few grace minutes so you can top up, then it ends cleanly.

Who owns the footage?

You do. We process it only to run your broadcast. Camera recordings kept for replays are deleted automatically after 3 days; highlight clips you save stay until you delete them. See the privacy policy.
