Questions

What organisers ask us.

What kinds of events can I stream?

Anything with people and phones: tournaments and league matches, weddings and family functions, concerts and fests, conferences and launches, religious events, school and college events. If you can film it on a phone, you can broadcast it.

Do I need special cameras or equipment?

No. Any recent Android phone or iPhone works as a camera. A tripod or clamp helps keep the shot steady. The director needs one phone or tablet.

What internet do the cameras need?

About 2.5 Mbps upload per camera at 720p on 4G, or 1.5 Mbps on a weak signal. With cloud mixing nothing else at the venue needs a connection.

Do viewers need to install anything?

No. They watch on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or your watch page in any browser.

Is it really live?

The director sees the cameras with under a second of delay. Viewers on YouTube and Facebook are usually 10 to 30 seconds behind, which is normal for those platforms.

Does the video go on my own YouTube channel?

Yes. You connect your channel once and every broadcast appears there, so the views, subscribers and recording are yours.

What happens if my credits run out mid-show?

The director is warned at 10 minutes and again at 2 minutes left. If credits reach zero the show keeps going for a few grace minutes so you can top up, then it ends cleanly.

Who owns the footage?

You do. We process it only to run your broadcast. Camera recordings kept for replays are deleted automatically after 3 days; highlight clips you save stay until you delete them. See the privacy policy.
