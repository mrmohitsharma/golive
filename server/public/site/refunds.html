Refunds and cancellations

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

In short

  • If you were charged but did not get your credits, or something on our side went wrong, we refund you or restore your credits.
  • Refunds reach your original payment method within 7 to 10 working days of approval.
  • Unused credits are not refunded: they never expire, so you can use them on any future event.
  • There is no subscription to cancel. You only pay when you buy a credit pack.

1. What you buy

{{BRAND}} is sold as prepaid credits in packs, priced in Indian rupees (INR) plus GST as shown at checkout. Credits are added to your club's wallet as soon as the payment goes through, and are used for live minutes, extra cameras, viewers and add-ons such as your own logo. Each event includes free cameras and free minutes; setting up, testing and rehearsing are free. Prices are on our pricing section.

2. When we refund

  • Charged but no credits. If your bank or UPI app was debited but credits did not reach your wallet within 1 hour, we add the credits or refund the full amount, as you prefer. Most of these sort themselves out automatically once the payment gateway confirms.
  • Charged twice or charged the wrong amount. We refund the extra amount in full.
  • A fault on our side. If our service stopped or broke a broadcast, or charged credits wrongly, we restore the credits used. If you would rather have money back for that purchase, we refund the unused part of it.
  • Where the law requires a refund.

3. When we do not refund

  • Unused credits, once a payment has gone through correctly. They never expire and are shared by all events of your club.
  • Credits used during a broadcast that ran as expected, including when it was affected by things outside our control: mobile or venue internet, phones, batteries, or YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.
  • Accounts closed for breaking our Terms of service.

Credits cannot be transferred to another club or exchanged for cash.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email {{EMAIL}} or open a support request within 30 days of the payment, with your club name, the order number from your receipt, and what went wrong. We reply within 2 working days.

5. Timeline

Once we approve a refund, we start it the same or next working day through Cashfree Payments, back to the method you paid with (UPI, card or net banking). It reaches your account within 7 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. If a refund includes GST, we issue a credit note with it. Restored credits are added to your wallet straight away.

6. Cancelling

  • Payments. If you leave the payment page before paying, nothing is charged. A payment that has gone through cannot be cancelled, but the refund rules above apply.
  • Events and broadcasts. You can cancel or end an event at any time. Credits are only used while you are live and for extras you added; ending early uses no more.
  • Your account. You can stop using {{BRAND}} and ask us to delete your account at any time. Unused credits are not refunded when you close your account.
