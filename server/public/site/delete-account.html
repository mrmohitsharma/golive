What we delete and what we keep

DeletedKept, and why
  • Your name, email and password
  • Organisation details, members and join codes
  • Events, player lists, scores and graphics settings
  • Recordings, highlight clips and watch-page chat
  • YouTube and Facebook tokens, Instagram stream keys, and comments copied from those platforms

Remove access to your YouTube channel

In the app, open Organisation and tap Disconnect next to YouTube. We delete the stored tokens and ask Google to revoke our access. You can also remove {{BRAND}} at any time from your Google Account at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Facebook and Instagram data deletion

In the app, open Organisation and tap Disconnect next to Facebook; we delete the stored Page tokens straight away. You can also remove {{BRAND}} from Facebook: go to Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Business Integrations, select {{BRAND}} and tap Remove. To ask us to delete everything we received from Facebook or Instagram, email {{EMAIL}} with the subject "Facebook data deletion" and the name of your Page; we delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Volunteers and viewers

Camera operators, scorers and commentators who joined with a code have no account. To remove your name from an event, ask the organiser, or email us with the event name and the name you used. Viewers can ask the organiser or us to remove their chat messages.

More detail is in our Privacy policy.
