Get your first event on air

  1. Direct from the web or the app. On a computer, open the control room. On a phone or tablet, install the app and log in with this account.

    In the app: Sign in → Scan QR from website. Works once, for 5:00.

  2. Connect your channels (optional). Your watch page plays the show with our own player, so YouTube is not required. To stream there too: on the web, Channels; in the app, tap the people icon and Connect YouTube or Facebook. For Instagram you paste a stream key per event.
  3. Create an event and choose Cloud production, so no laptop is needed.
  4. Share the camera QR code with the phones at the venue. Each one taps Go live.
  5. Go live from the control room. Your watch page link and the player embed code are on the event's Streaming tab, ready to share.
