Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

{{BRAND}} lets organisers broadcast live events with phones as cameras, mix the show in the cloud and stream it to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. This policy explains what information we handle to do that, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, so it uses plain language.

The short version. We use your information only to run your broadcasts and your account. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models. When you connect YouTube or Facebook we use that access only to start, run, end and read comments on the broadcasts you create with us.

  • To create and secure your account and let your team and volunteers join your events.
  • To run broadcasts: receive camera video, mix the programme, render graphics, record short replays, make clips, and stream to the platforms you choose.
  • To show live scores, leaderboards and the combined chat on your broadcast and watch page.
  • To measure live minutes and cameras, charge credits, process purchases and issue receipts.
  • To keep the service safe: prevent abuse and spam, enforce bans, investigate faults and fix bugs.
  • To contact you about your account, payments, important service changes and support requests. We send marketing email only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

We rely on your consent when you create an account, connect a platform or start a broadcast, and on the legitimate uses the law allows for things like security, fraud prevention and legal compliance. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 12); this stops future processing but does not undo what was already done lawfully.

5. Google user data and YouTube API Services

{{BRAND}} uses the YouTube API Services. If you connect a YouTube channel, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we ask for and why

When an organisation owner taps Connect YouTube, Google asks you to grant the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube permission ("Manage your YouTube account"). We need this permission because YouTube's live-streaming features (creating a live broadcast, creating a stream, binding them and starting or ending the broadcast) are only available with it. We use it to:

  • read your channel's ID and title, so you can see which channel is connected;
  • create a live broadcast and a live stream for each event you choose to stream, with the title and privacy setting you pick;
  • start and end that broadcast when your director taps Go live and End stream, and read its status and stream health;
  • read the live chat messages of that broadcast, so they appear in the combined chat for your commentator.

We do not use this access to read, upload, edit or delete your other videos, playlists, subscriptions or comments, to post anything other than the broadcasts you start, or to access any other Google service such as Gmail, Drive or Contacts.

How we store and protect it

  • Google gives us an access token and a refresh token. The refresh token is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Access tokens are kept only in server memory and expire within an hour.
  • We store your channel ID and title, and the IDs of broadcasts we create, for as long as the channel stays connected.
  • Live chat messages copied from your YouTube broadcasts are deleted from our servers automatically within 30 days.

Sharing and human access

We do not sell Google user data, and we do not share or transfer it to anyone except: our hosting provider, which stores it on our behalf under contract; when you direct us to (for example, by showing a viewer's comment on your broadcast); or when the law requires it. We never use it for advertising, credit decisions or training AI or machine-learning models. Our staff do not read it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or abuse investigations, or the law requires it.

{{BRAND}}'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and revoking access

You can disconnect YouTube at any time in the app (Organisation, then Disconnect next to YouTube). We then delete the stored tokens and ask Google to revoke our access. You can also revoke access directly from your Google Account at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Broadcasts already published stay on your YouTube channel, where you control them.

Sign in with Google. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and a Google account ID with us (the openid, email and profile permissions only). We use them only to create and sign in to your {{BRAND}} account. We do not get your Google password or access to any other Google data, and we keep these details until you delete your account.

6. Facebook and Instagram data

If you connect Facebook, Meta asks you to grant these permissions. We use each one only as described:

  • pages_show_list: list the Pages you manage, so you can choose one to stream to.
  • pages_read_engagement: read basic information about that Page and the status of live videos we create.
  • pages_manage_posts: create, start and end the live video on that Page for your event.
  • pages_read_user_content: read comments on those live videos, so they appear in the combined chat.

Page access tokens are encrypted at rest. Comments copied from Facebook are deleted from our servers automatically within 30 days. You can disconnect Facebook in the app, which deletes the stored tokens, and remove {{BRAND}} from your Facebook settings under Business Integrations. Meta's handling of your data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Instagram. Meta does not let apps start Instagram live videos, so you copy a stream key from Instagram and paste it into the app for each event. We encrypt that key, use it only to send your broadcast to Instagram, and you can replace or remove it at any time. Instagram keys stop working after one live video.

To ask us to delete data we received from Facebook or Instagram, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

7. Payments

Credit purchases are processed by Cashfree Payments India Pvt Ltd on its own secure checkout page. You enter card, UPI or bank details with Cashfree, not with us, and we never receive or store them. Cashfree tells us the order number, amount and whether the payment succeeded. Cashfree's handling of your data is covered by its own privacy policy.

8. Who we share information with

  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us under contract and only on our instructions: Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage and computing) and Cashfree (payments).
  • The platforms you choose. When you stream to YouTube, Facebook or Instagram, we send your broadcast and its title to that platform. What happens there is governed by that platform's terms and policies.
  • Your organisation. Owners and admins of your organisation can see its members, events, credits and usage.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, a court order or a lawful request from authorities, or to protect people's safety and our rights.
  • Business transfers. If {{BRAND}} is merged or sold, information may move to the new owner, who must respect this policy.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

9. What is public

A broadcast is public by design. Anyone can see the live video on the platforms you stream to, and, if the organiser turns on the watch page, the video, player names, scores, leaderboard, highlight clips the organiser marks public, and chat messages with the names viewers chose. Viewers should not post anything in chat they would not want others to see.

11. Security

Traffic between our website, app and servers is encrypted in transit with HTTPS, and camera streams are sent over SRT with per-camera access keys and encryption. Passwords are stored only as hashes, and platform tokens and stream keys are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

12. Your rights and choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other laws that apply to you, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, or delete your account (see how to delete your data);
  • withdraw consent, for example by disconnecting YouTube or Facebook;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied with our answer, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Email {{EMAIL}} from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we reply within 30 days. Volunteers and viewers who want their name or messages removed from an event can also ask the organiser.

13. Children

Accounts are for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. Organisers who broadcast events involving children, such as school sports days, are responsible for getting verifiable consent from a parent, guardian or the school before filming or showing a child's name, and for not tracking, profiling or targeting children. If you believe a child's data was shared with us without that consent, email us and we will remove it.

14. Where data is processed

Our servers are hosted by Amazon Web Services. When you stream to YouTube, Facebook or Instagram, Google and Meta process your broadcast in their own data centres, which may be outside India. We transfer data outside India only as permitted by Indian law.
