Phone cameras

Any phone becomes a pro camera.

Volunteers scan a QR code and tap Go live. No account, no app setup, any recent Android phone or iPhone.

Pro controls under the thumbs

  • Brightness arc on the left, zoom arc on the right, with .5 · 1 · 2 · 5 shortcuts
  • Ultra-wide 0.5× lens where the phone has one
  • Tap to focus and set brightness there; long-press to lock them
  • Auto or manual focus, and the torch for evening events

Made for long shows

  • Pocket mode: black screen, preview off, still streaming: saves the most battery
  • Screen dims by itself when nobody touches it
  • Padlock freezes every control against pocket touches
  • Landscape either way round; portrait 9:16 when you want vertical

Sound you can trust

  • Live mic meter, and a mic button to mute (the director sees MIC OFF)
  • A reminder that the mic is on when you go live
  • Red frame on the phone when it is on air, green when it is next

Control room

Direct the show from a phone, tablet or browser.

Every camera in one place. Pick the next shot, take it to air, go live: the buttons light up one after another so first-timers never get lost.

Switching

  • A small NEXT box, a big ON AIR picture, and TAKE in between
  • Cut straight to any camera with a double-tap
  • Step-by-step Go live buttons with hints on top

What you see of each phone

  • Live thumbnails, battery, upload speed and heat
  • ON A CALL, PAUSED and MIC OFF badges as they happen
  • Rename cameras and assign them to a hole, stage or court

Cloud OBS or Laptop OBS

No laptop at the venue. Or use yours.

Cloud OBS mixes your show on our servers, so the venue needs no computer and no strong internet. Prefer your own setup? Laptop OBS runs on your computer with your scenes.

Cloud OBS

  • Ready in about a minute from Start show
  • Graphics, replays, sound and switching all from the control room
  • Turns itself off 2 minutes after the show ends, or when nothing is live, with a warning and a Keep running button

Laptop OBS

  • Your own OBS Studio with a scene per camera, set up automatically
  • Sends 720p, the sweet spot for phones on 4G
  • Switch between Cloud and Laptop any time before going live

Watch page and platforms

Your own watch page, plus everywhere else.

Pick a main stream and add as many places as you like. Pause or switch the main one at any time.

Your watch page

  • Our own ad-free player, delivered through a fast global network
  • Live video, chat, leaderboard, clips and games on one link
  • Embed it on your club or company website with one paste

Every platform at once

  • YouTube (your channel, ours, or a stream key), your Facebook Page, Instagram
  • Any service with a stream URL and key: LinkedIn, Twitch, Kick…
  • Vertical 9:16 for phone-first platforms

Stays on air

Built for venues with weak signal and busy volunteers.

Things go wrong at live events. The show keeps going.

When something goes wrong

  • Phones reconnect by themselves when the signal drops
  • If the camera on air drops, the show switches to a "back soon" screen
  • If a camera operator gets a phone call, the show switches to another camera and the director is told
  • After a call the camera comes back on its own

No surprise bills

  • Pay per live minute, warnings before credits run out
  • Cloud OBS and phone uploads stop when the show ends
